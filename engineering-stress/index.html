Reference

Stress units

  • 1 Pa = 1 N/m²
  • 1 kPa = 1 kN/m²
  • 1 MPa = 1 N/mm²
  • 1 GPa = 1 kN/mm²

Area formulas

  • Rectangle: A = bh
  • Square: A = s²
  • Circle: A = πr² = πd²/4
  • Hollow rectangle: A = BH − bh
  • Hollow square: A = S² − s²
  • Hollow circle: A = π(D² − d²)/4
  • Plate with hole: outer area − hole area

Engineering habit

Check that all dimensions use the same unit before calculating an area. Then check that your stress unit matches the force and area units you used.
