Dot Product Foundations

The dot product combines two vectors using corresponding components. The symbol is a dot, ·, not a multiplication cross ×.

There is one important difference from the vector operations you have already used: the answer to a dot product is a scalar — a number.

3. Connect dot product and cosine

The dot product, the two magnitudes, and the angle are connected by one relationship.

a · b = |a| |b| cos θ

Because |a| and |b| are scalar magnitudes, writing them beside each other means ordinary multiplication.

For substitution: a · b = |a| × |b| × cos θ
